# Views

Choose a view with the `currentView` prop. It defaults to `MONTH`.

```jsx
<Calendar currentView="WEEK_TIME" /* ... */ />
```

| View | Layout | Navigation step | `weekStartsOn` | `cellDisplayMode` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`MONTH`](#month) | Days of the month in weeks | 1 month | ✓ | ✓ |
| [`WEEK`](#week) | One week of day cells | 1 week | ✓ | ✓ |
| [`WEEK_TIME`](#week_time) | One week with an hour timeline | 1 week | ✓ | |
| [`DAY`](#day) | One day with an hour timeline | 1 day | | |
| [`DAY_REVERSE`](#day_reverse) | One day, hours as columns | 1 day | | |
| [`WEEK_IN_PLACE`](#week_in_place) | One week, items grouped by hour | 1 week | ✓ | ✓ |
| [`DAY_IN_PLACE`](#day_in_place) | One day, items grouped by hour | 1 day | | ✓ |

Every view supports [color dots](/color-dots), the built-in navigation and
[custom rendering](/custom-rendering) of items.

## MONTH

The whole month in weekly rows. Items that span several days are drawn as one bar
across those days. Good for an overview of busy periods and important dates.

To stay fast with many items, the month view draws the items of a week when that row
scrolls into view. It uses `IntersectionObserver` for this, which every modern browser
has; in tests, see [the FAQ](/faq#tests-crash-or-show-no-items-in-the-month-view).

## WEEK

One week of day cells, laid out like a single row of the month view. Good for
planning by week without the noise of exact times.

<ViewDemo view="WEEK" />

## WEEK_TIME

One week with a vertical timeline of hours. Items are sized by their duration, and
overlapping items sit side by side. Items longer than 24 hours move to a row above the
timeline, with arrows when they continue from the previous week or into the next one.

<ViewDemo view="WEEK_TIME" />

## DAY

A single day with the same hour timeline as `WEEK_TIME`, including the row for items
longer than 24 hours. Good for a detailed daily schedule.

<ViewDemo view="DAY" />

## DAY_REVERSE

A single day where hours run horizontally as columns. An item that spans several days
is drawn on each day it covers. Good for wide layouts such as resource or room
timelines.

<ViewDemo view="DAY_REVERSE" height={300} />

## WEEK_IN_PLACE

One week where each day has a cell per hour. Items are grouped into the hour in which
they **start**; their duration is not drawn. Good for seeing how many things begin at
which hour, like a heat map of activity.

<ViewDemo view="WEEK_IN_PLACE" />

## DAY_IN_PLACE

The same grouping by start hour as `WEEK_IN_PLACE`, for a single day.
